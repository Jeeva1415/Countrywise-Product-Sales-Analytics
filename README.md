# Countrywise Product Sales Analytics

**Tools:** Power BI, Excel, Data Visualization

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes 700+ sales records across 5 countries and 6 product lines, tracking **$4.69M in revenue** and **$2.76M in profit**.

## Key Insights
- **Canada** is the top revenue-generating market (~$1.03M).
- **Chocolate Chip** is the highest-profit product line (~$1.01M).
- These findings can guide regional and product strategy.

## Data Preparation
- Cleaned and modeled multi-year (2018-2019) sales data.
- Ensured accurate country-level and product-level reporting.
